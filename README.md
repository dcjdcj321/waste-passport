# waste-passport
A national Property Waste Passport platform that reduces small-scale fly-tipping by introducing accountability at the point where waste is created, rather than focusing solely on waste carriers and disposal facilities.

# The Problem
Current UK fly-tipping controls have several weaknesses:
- Most fly-tipping originates from household, renovation and tenancy-clearance activities.
- Local authority reporting is fragmented across hundreds of council websites.
- Citizens often don't know which council owns the land where waste is dumped.
- Landlords, renovators and homeowners have little evidence that waste was disposed of correctly.
- Rogue waste carriers can collect waste and illegally dump it with limited risk of detection.
- Enforcement is largely reactive and depends on eyewitness evidence.

# Proposed Solution
## Property Waste Passport
A digital record attached to a property and its waste-producing activities.

Every waste-producing event can be registered:
- Kitchen replacement
- Bathroom replacement
- House clearance
- End-of-tenancy clearance
- Garden clearance
- Small building works
- White goods disposal
- Furniture disposal
The platform creates a unique Waste Passport ID.

# Stakeholders

# Waste Carrier

# Disposal Site
Confirms:
- Receipt
- Weight

# Community Members

- Scan Waste Passport QR codes
- Verify registration status
Without accessing personal information.
